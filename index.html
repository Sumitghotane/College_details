ITR-3

For Business / Profession

Applicable to Individuals & HUFs having income from any of the following:

  • Business or Profession — proprietorship, partnership firm, LLP
  • Presumptive Business Income — Section 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE
  • Income from Capital Gains
  • Income from More than One House Property
  • Foreign Assets / Foreign Income
  • Income from other sources — interest, dividend, etc.
  • Expenditure other than interest exceeding ₹2,00,000
  • Total Income exceeds the Basic Exemption Limit
ITR-4

For Presumptive Income

Applicable to Individuals & HUFs satisfying the relevant presumptive-income conditions:

  • Presumptive Business under Section 44AD, 44ADA or 44AE
  • Total Turnover / Gross Receipts up to ₹2 Crore
  • ₹3 Crore in case of 44AD as per new tax-regime conditions
  • No Income from Capital Gains
  • No Income from more than one House Property
  • No Foreign Assets / Foreign Income
  • Income from other sources, if any, within the permissible limit
